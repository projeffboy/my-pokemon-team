# 8. Assumptions and Interview

These documents were first written from the repository alone: the code, the tests, `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `TODO.md`, the update log, and the old website's source. That is an analysis of an existing system. It cannot tell what the Site Owner wants, only what the site does. Every place where a document needed something the repository does not hold is marked **[A-n]**, and this document lists those assumptions, each with the question that settles it.

Section 8.2 is the interview script. Section 8.3 records the answers and what was changed because of them.

## 8.2 Interview script

- **Interviewee**: the Site Owner, who is also the Maintainer and the Developer (SH-6, SH-2, SH-5).
- **Goal**: confirm or correct the business requirements, the scope, and the priorities, so that Baseline 1.0 can be approved.
- **Preparation done**: the existing system was analysed, and the draft documents are the background for the questions.
- **Notes**: answers are recorded in Section 8.3 in the interviewee's own words, and the documents are corrected soon after.

The questions go from open to specific. Indirect questions come first, so that the stated needs are not shaped by the draft's wording.

### Customers, goals, and benefits

- **Q1.** Why did you make the site, and what do you want from it now? What would make you proud of it in a year?
- **Q2.** What does the site cost to run each month, and what does advertising bring in? Is covering costs enough, or is there an income you are aiming for?
- **Q3.** How will you judge that the redesign has succeeded? What would make you take the "Beta" label off, and what would make you retire the old website?
- **Q4.** Which other teambuilders do your players use or compare the site with? What do they do better?

### The problem and its solution

- **Q5.** What is not part of the plan for the site? Of battling, damage calculation, accounts, cloud storage of teams, and suggested pokemon, which are out for good and which are only out for now?
- **Q6.** When a new game, regulation, or Showdown data change comes out, how soon do players expect it on the site? When a player reports a wrong result, how soon should it be fixed?
- **Q7.** What are your constraints: the hours you can give in a week, what you are willing to pay each month, and anything the hosting or ad platform forbids?
- **Q8.** What worries you most: Showdown changing its terms, ad revenue falling, players losing teams, a rights holder objecting, or something not on the list?

### Users

- **Q9.** Who writes to you through feedback, and what do they ask for? Which of these do you recognize: an in-game player on a phone, a Smogon singles player on a desktop, a doubles player who keeps many teams in another language, a returning player of older generations? Who is missing?

### Specific questions

- **Q10.** The site already computes whether a team breaks its format's rules but does not show it. Should it? And should a player be told when an old link has lost a pokemon or move to a data update?
- **Q11.** For moving a team between the old website and the redesigned site: is carrying the current team across enough, or do saved teams need to cross too?
- **Q12.** Where a new feature makes the site better for players and harder for you to maintain, which wins? More generations, languages, and games each did that.
- **Q13.** On a mid-range phone, how fast is fast enough: for the analysis to follow a change, for the list of pokemon to open, and for a shared link to show its team? How long should a first-time visitor need to get a team analysed?
- **Q14.** How many players visit in a day, and at the busiest moment? Has the site ever been slow or unavailable because of traffic?
- **Q15.** Which browsers and devices do your players use? Do Firefox and Edge matter?
- **Q16.** Of the planned work (battle format for the checklist, team transfer between versions, Let's Go and the Legends games, legality warnings, a verified sender domain for feedback, a notice of dropped entries), what would you do first, and what would you drop?

### Calibration and tracking

- **Q17.** Is there anyone else whose view should be asked: a co-maintainer, a translator, a player you trust?
- **Q18.** You asked for "something else" besides the vision, the models, the specification, and the task list. What is it?
- **Q19.** Is there anything I should have asked and did not?
