# Stat icons

`StatIcon.tsx` recreates the six stat symbols from the [official Pokémon Champions training screenshot](https://champions.pokemon.com/_images/pokemon/train-pokemon/en/p04_01.jpg), shown on the [Pokémon page](https://champions.pokemon.com/en-us/pokemon/).

The vectors use the site's text color so they work in both light and dark themes. The defense symbols use outlined shields to keep their inner symbols legible at 20px.
